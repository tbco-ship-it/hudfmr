Data & method

Source. HUD User, Fair Market Rents: the full FMR history file ({{ source.fmr_file }}) and the {{ fy }} Small Area FMR schedule ({{ source.safmr_file }}), downloaded {{ source.fetched }}. Public domain.

Areas. One page per FMR row: 3,161 counties and county equivalents plus 1,603 New England towns that HUD rates individually. Counties inside a metro share the metro FMR; the page names the metro area it belongs to.

ZIP codes. One page per ZIP with a published SAFMR. A ZIP that HUD lists in more than one FMR area shows the entry for each area; "% vs area" compares the ZIP's SAFMR with the FMR of the first county in that area. Each ZIP page offers its rows as data.json.

Change. Percent change is {{ fy }} versus {{ fy_prev }} for the same row. The 11-year table is taken from the HUD FMR history file retrieved on the date shown above; it reflects that file's version and may incorporate HUD revisions — we do not reconstruct each fiscal year's original release.

Payment standards. 90% and 110% of FMR/SAFMR, HUD's basic range. Your housing authority's actual standard is on its website.

Refresh. HUD publishes the next fiscal year's FMRs in late summer; the site is rebuilt when they land.
