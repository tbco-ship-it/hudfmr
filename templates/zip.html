How to use this number

A Housing Choice Voucher pays the payment standard (90–110% of the figure above, set by the local housing authority) minus about 30% of the household's adjusted monthly income. If the unit rents for more than the standard, the tenant pays the difference, capped at 40% of income at move-in. Landlords: the standard is the ceiling most voucher tenants can meet, so pricing at or under it fills a unit faster. Payment standards explained
