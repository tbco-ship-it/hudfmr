What the number is

Fair Market Rent is HUD's estimate of the 40th-percentile gross rent for a modest, recently rented unit in an area — the rent at which 40% of units go for less. It resets every October 1 and is the yardstick for Housing Choice (Section 8) vouchers, LIHTC rent caps and several state programs. Why the ZIP number differs from the county's

In most large metros HUD now publishes Small Area FMRs by ZIP code, so a voucher stretches further in a higher-rent ZIP. Where a ZIP has no SAFMR, the metro-wide FMR applies. Either way the housing authority sets a payment standard between 90% and 110% of the figure, and your share of rent depends on that standard, not the FMR itself. How payment standards work
