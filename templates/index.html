What the number is

Fair Market Rent is HUD's estimate of the 40th-percentile gross rent for a modest, recently rented unit in an area — the rent at which 40% of units go for less. It resets every October 1 and is the yardstick for Housing Choice (Section 8) vouchers and several state programs (LIHTC rent limits use HUD's separate MTSP income limits). Why the ZIP number differs from the county's

In most large metros HUD now publishes Small Area FMRs by ZIP code, so a voucher stretches further in a higher-rent ZIP. Where a ZIP has no SAFMR, the metro-wide FMR applies. Either way the housing authority sets its payment standard, normally between 90% and 110% of the figure (exceptions exist), and your share of rent depends on that standard and your income, not the FMR itself.
