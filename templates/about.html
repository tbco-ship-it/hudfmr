About FMR Finder

HUD publishes Fair Market Rents as spreadsheets and a lookup form that returns one area at a time. FMR Finder turns the same {{ fy }} schedule into a page per county, town and ZIP code with all five bedroom sizes, the change from last year, the 90–110% payment-standard range, and the areas around it — for tenants sizing a voucher, landlords pricing a unit, and anyone comparing rents across the country.
